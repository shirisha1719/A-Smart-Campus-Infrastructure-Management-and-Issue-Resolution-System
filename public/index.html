Smart Campus Maintenance Management

Built from the ground up for university infrastructure—bridging students, maintenance technicians, and administrators in real time.

Interactive Campus Map

Pinpoint maintenance issues with exact campus building coordinates, floor levels, and specific room landmarks so technicians locate faults immediately.

Live 4-Step Stepper Tracker

Students see exact progress step-by-step: Submitted ➔ Assigned ➔ In Progress ➔ Resolved, with automated notification pings.

Specialized Staff Dispatch

Administrators triage complaints and assign them directly to technicians categorized by skill: Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, WiFi/Network, or Civil.

Resolution Proof Photos

Maintain high work quality with mandatory before-and-after photographic verification uploaded directly by technicians upon completing tasks.

Instant Socket.IO Updates

WebSockets ensure student status updates, admin ticket feeds, and staff work order counters reflect live without reloading the browser.

Student Quality Reviews

Once resolved, reporting students submit 1-to-5 star feedback and reviews, ensuring continuous service accountability and university excellence.

SEAMLESS LIFECYCLE

How Fix My Campus Works

Three easy steps to transform broken facilities into rapid, high-quality resolutions.

1

Spot & Submit

Student or faculty spots an issue, snaps a photo, selects the campus building and room, and submits a ticket in under 30 seconds.

2

Triage & Dispatch

Campus administrator assesses priority level and assigns the ticket to the qualified maintenance technician with specific notes.

3

Fix, Verify & Rate

Staff completes repair, uploads proof photograph, and student receives instant notification to verify and leave star feedback.
